Businesses generate huge amounts of sales data every day. However, managers often struggle to quickly identify trends, top-performing products, customer behavior, and revenue opportunities. This project provides interactive dashboards and an AI assistant that delivers insights through natural language questions.

Questions to perform in the end 


Which city generated the highest revenue?
Which products are least profitable?
Which category has the highest sales?
Which month has the highest revenue?
Who are the top 10 customers?
Which region performs best?
Which products should be discontinued?
What is the average order value?
Which states generate the highest profit?
Which products should we promote?
